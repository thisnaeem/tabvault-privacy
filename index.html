TabVault — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

TabVault — Session Manager & Tab Saver is built to be private by construction: it has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and it never sends your data anywhere.

What TabVault stores

TabVault stores the following only on your device, in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB and chrome.storage.local):

  • Tab URLs, page titles, and favicon URLs — for the snapshots, sessions, and closed-window records you see in the app.
  • Tab order, pinned state, and tab-group titles/colors, so restores match how your windows were arranged.
  • Your settings (snapshot interval, retention periods, theme).

TabVault never stores page content, form data, passwords, cookies, or browsing data from private (incognito) windows — incognito windows are never captured at all.

What TabVault does NOT do

  • It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
  • It has no analytics, tracking, ads, or third-party code phoning home.
  • It does not use remote code. All code is bundled in the extension.
  • It declares no host permissions and injects no content scripts, so it cannot read the pages you visit.

Permissions, one by one

PermissionWhy TabVault needs it
tabsList your open tabs (URL/title) to snapshot them and to reopen tabs when you restore a session.
tabGroupsRead group titles/colors and recreate groups on restore.
storageSave your settings in chrome.storage.local.
alarmsSchedule periodic automatic snapshots.
unlimitedStorageKeep snapshot history in IndexedDB without Chrome evicting it under storage pressure.
sidePanelShow the TabVault side panel.
faviconDisplay site icons for saved tabs via Chrome's built-in favicon service.

Deleting your data

  • Delete individual snapshots, sessions, and recovery records in the Manager at any time.
  • Automatic history is pruned after the retention period you set (default 7 days; closed windows 14 days).
  • Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.
